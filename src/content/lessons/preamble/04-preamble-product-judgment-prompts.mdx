You have a page whose button changes a message. Suppose someone does not notice the new message. Today you will ask Codex to make it clearer, then open the page and decide whether the result helps.

Click this example. The changed message gets more space and a stronger visual cue. Your version can look different if it makes the result easy to notice.

<PreamblePageDemo stage={4} />

A useful request is short enough that you can picture the outcome:

```text
In my button page, make the message after the click easier to notice.
Keep the button and its current action. Make a small change and tell me
what to look for when I open the page.
```

Before sending it, imagine what “easier to notice” might mean: a stronger color, a little space, or clearer wording. Codex may choose one. You can ask why if the choice surprises you.

After Codex edits the file, open it in the browser. Click the button. Does the new message catch your eye? If it does not, describe what you see and ask for one smaller adjustment. If the button stops working, tell Codex what happened and include any Console error.
